Telegram Dockerfile installs [PyTgCalls](https://github.com/pytgcalls/pytgcalls)
and [NTgCalls](https://github.com/pytgcalls/ntgcalls), which distribute their
own LGPL license terms. No pre-built image or upstream binary is included in
this source release. Review the exact dependency versions and comply with
their applicable notice and source obligations before redistributing a built
Telegram image.
